Karyotype

Chromosomes are simplified drawings. Photo style shows each chromosome as one rod, the way it usually looks under a light microscope; the other style shows its two sister chromatids. Their relative lengths and centromere positions follow real human chromosomes; banding patterns are stylized. Patient cases are fictional.
